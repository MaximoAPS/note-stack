## Dataset Information

- **Website**: https://magenta.tensorflow.org/datasets/maestro
- **Version**: 3.0.0
- **Description**: MAESTRO is a dataset composed of over 200 hours of virtuosic piano performances captured with fine alignment between note labels and audio waveforms.
