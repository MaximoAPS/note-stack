## Included Pieces

This repository includes four short excerpts from MAESTRO v3.0.0:

1. **scarlatti-sonata.mid** - Domenico Scarlatti: Sonata K. 525 (~66s, test split, 2008)
2. **liszt-preludio.mid** - Franz Liszt: Transcendental Etude No. 1 "Preludio" (~45s, validation split, 2015)
3. **mendelssohn-etude.mid** - Felix Mendelssohn: Etude in A Minor (~76s, validation split, 2008)
4. **chopin-etude.mid** - Frédéric Chopin: Etude Op. 10 No. 2 (~71s, train split, 2013)

## Dataset Information

- **Website**: https://magenta.tensorflow.org/datasets/maestro
- **Version**: 3.0.0
- **Description**: MAESTRO is a dataset composed of over 200 hours of virtuosic piano performances captured with fine alignment between note labels and audio waveforms.
